How It Works

Simple for communities,
powerful under the hood

01
🏗️

Create or Join a Group

Start a new Stokvel, Chama, or Harambee in minutes, or join an existing one with an invite code. Set contribution rules and payout schedules together.

02
💰

Contribute Together

Members contribute to the shared wallet on schedule. Every transaction is logged in real-time. See exactly who has contributed and how much.

⚡

When it's your turn or an emergency arises, funds are released instantly — verified with biometrics and approved by the group.
